El Glosario Perdido
As you walk through an industrial park at night, you kick a can of spray paint, pick it up, take two steps, and fall through solid concrete, noclipping into something vast. Endless buzzing hallways, moist carpet, no sky. Just yellow.
In your pocket: a spray can, a lighter, and your old Spanish glosario. Its pages tore loose in the fall and now drift through the halls as glowing vocabulary cards. Find every word, read it aloud, and each one restores your VIDA. Restore the glosario to 100% and you will un-noclip, slipping back out of the Backrooms and into reality.

HOW TO SURVIVE
MOVE
Left thumb joystick to walk, drag the right side to look (WASD + arrows on keyboard).
Sprint boosts speed (watch the ring). The walk button locks you moving forward.
PALABRAS / WORDS
Glowing cards float through the halls. Walk into one to collect it, then say the word aloud.
Every new word restores VIDA. Open the MENU to see your inventory, save, or change settings.
FLAMETHROWER
Hold the spray button to burn anything that gets close. Entities are rare and go down fast.
Tap it near a wall to tag it. Red cans refill your paint.
THE WAY OUT
There is no door. Restore your glosario to 100% (every last page) and you will un-noclip and land back in reality. If your VIDA runs out, you simply wake up and keep going.
